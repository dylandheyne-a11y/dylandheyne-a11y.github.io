What these numbers mean

45 hires/year against a benchmark of 30 isn't just volume — it's volume with a 55% proactive source rate, meaning more than half of every hire came from targeted outreach, not applications. That's a different and harder kind of recruiting.

100% at COO/ET-1 review means every executive-level candidate write-up I authored was approved at the final stage. These write-ups synthesized talent market density, work trial performance, AI fluency, and panel conviction — a format I developed and owned end-to-end.

The 87% offer acceptance rate was maintained in part by tracking offer loss patterns systematically and partnering with Comp to expand pay bands on roles where comp was a recurring close blocker.
